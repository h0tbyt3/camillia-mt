### New
- The channel list is now a drawer that slides in from the left over the chat. It stays open until you pick a channel, then slides back. This applies to every board except the T-Lora Pager TFT and the T-Display P4 in landscape, which keep the list beside the chat.
- On touchscreen boards (Heltec V4, Wio Tracker L2, T-Display P4 in portrait, and the T-Deck, T-Deck Pro and Mesh Deck touchscreens), open the channel list by tapping the channel name at the top of the chat screen or by swiping in from the very left edge of the screen.
- To close the channel list without switching channels, tap anywhere outside it or swipe it back to the left. The chat behind it dims while it is open.
- The Help screen now tells you how to open the channel list on the board you are holding.

### Changed
- The channel name at the top of the chat screen is now plain header text, left-aligned and in the same style as the clock. It no longer has a button outline, glow or dropdown arrow.
- Unread channels no longer make the channel name in the header pulse. Unread status now shows on the channels in the drawer.
- On T-Deck, T-Deck Pro and Mesh Deck, press C on the chat screen to open the channel list. On the M9, press Messages again on the chat screen. On the Cardputer, H opens and closes it.
- On T-Deck Pro, the channel drawer appears and disappears in one step instead of sliding, to avoid extra e-paper refreshes. It covers the full height of the screen, with a clear border.
- The channel list has more space around its channels and grows wider to fit long channel names.
