### New
- T-Display P4: the keyboard expansion's backlight now has a brightness setting (Low, Medium, High or Off), on the device and in the web config page.
- T-Display P4: F11 on the keyboard expansion now steps the keyboard light through Low, Medium, High and Off. If F7 had switched the light off, F11 turns it back on.

### Changed
- T-Display P4: Help now opens with F10 instead of F11, and the bottom bar's key hint shows F10.
- T-Display P4: picking a keyboard light level in settings turns the light back on if F7 had switched it off.
