### New
- Spell suggestions while composing messages: after a word is finished, a row of corrections appears above the message box. Tab or the trackball/scroll wheel cycles through them and Enter keeps the choice. Typing on or leaving the row alone changes nothing. Available when the device language is English, on every supported board except the M5Stack Cardputer.
- New "Spell Check" on/off setting, in the device settings next to Language and on the web config page. It is on by default and takes effect immediately without a restart. In other languages the setting is kept and marked "(English only)".
