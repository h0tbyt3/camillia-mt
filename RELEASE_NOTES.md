### New
- T-Display P4: the keyboard expansion can now be clipped on or removed while the device is running and is detected within a couple of seconds. It no longer has to be attached at boot.
- T-Display P4: Caps Lock now lights the keyboard expansion's indicator LEDs.
- T-Display P4: F7 turns the keyboard expansion's backlight on or off, and the setting is kept across reboots. The backlight also turns off and on with the screen.
- T-Display P4: in landscape with the keyboard expansion attached, each navigation bar button shows the F-key that opens it.
- T-Display P4: with the keyboard expansion attached, the on-screen keyboard no longer appears in compose, channel editing, Wi-Fi password, node name, node filter, the admin terminal, or first-time setup. The compose box gets the extra space and shows twice as many lines.
- T-Display P4: the keyboard expansion's Left and Right arrows move the cursor inside text fields.
- T-Display P4: the keyboard expansion's Up and Down arrows move through the channel list and wrap at either end.
- T-Display P4: you can type into the Nodes filter dialog on the keyboard expansion. Enter applies the filter, and Backspace on an empty field closes it.

### Changed
- T-Display P4: the keyboard expansion's F-keys now follow the navigation bar from left to right: F1 Home, F2 Chat, F3 DMs, F4 Nodes, F5 Tools, F6 Config, F7 keyboard light, F11 Help.
- ThinkNode M9: the dedicated Map button now opens Nodes. Discovery is still available from Tools.

### Fixed
- T-Display P4: the keyboard expansion no longer stops responding after the audio hardware has used the shared bus.
- T-Display P4: the firmware no longer tries to switch to CPU speeds the chip doesn't support, which filled the log with repeated errors.
