### New
- T-Display P4 and Heltec V4 expansion display, held upright: every screen opened from Tools (Live, ChUtil, SNR/RSSI, Beacons, MQTT Monitor, Discovery, Weather) now has a centred title at the top and a row of large action buttons below it, in place of the old header bar.
- T-Display P4, held upright: the Tools menu lists its tools one per row as large full-width buttons, with the title at the top.
- Heltec V4 expansion display, held upright: the Tools menu title now sits at the top of the screen, above the two-column grid.

### Changed
- Touch devices: the Tools screens and the Tools menu no longer have a close X. Use the bottom nav bar instead. Tap Tools to go back to the list, or tap any other tab to go straight there.
- Touch devices: the ChUtil, SNR/RSSI, Beacons, MQTT Monitor and Discovery screens now show the bottom nav bar.
- Touch devices: action buttons such as Filter, Clear, Scan, Send and Reset now sit in a full-width row under the title instead of being squeezed into the header.
- MQTT Monitor on touch devices: the topic title now uses the full width of the screen.
- Live screen: the nav bar now highlights Tools, since Live is opened from Tools.
- T-Display P4, held upright: the Nodes list and node details use larger text and taller rows.
- T-Display P4, held upright: the direct message contact list uses larger text and taller rows.
- T-Display P4, held upright: the node cards on the home screen use larger text.

### Fixed
- Touch devices: tapping a nav bar tab from a Tools screen now closes that screen. Before, the screen stayed open underneath and came back when you left the new one.
